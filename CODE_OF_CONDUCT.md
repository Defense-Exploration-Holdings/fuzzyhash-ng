# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the technical subject, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to security@defense-exploration.com. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially. Say what happened, where (a link if there is one), and who was involved.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on reports following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: a private warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious the behaviour was and whether it continued after a warning. The maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code.
